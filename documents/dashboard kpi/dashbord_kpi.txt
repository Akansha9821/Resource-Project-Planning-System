Employee Utilization KPIs (Core for your project)

Q1: Utilization Rate
What it shows: % of employees actively working vs idle (bench)

Formula: Utilization Rate = (Employees with End Date filled / Total Employees) × 100

Insight:
Low value = high bench strength
High value = efficient workforce usage

Q2. Bench Strength Ratio
What it shows: Employees currently not assigned to projects
Logic:
If End Date = NULL → likely on bench
Bench Strength (%) = (Bench Employees / Total Employees) × 100

3. Average Bench Duration
What it shows: How long employees stay idle
Formula:
Bench Duration = Current Date – Last Project End Date
